# Alchemy Balance Reference

## Element Profiles (Apprentice Tier)

### Fire — Aggro & Burn
- **Identity**: Fast creatures (swift), direct damage (blast), removal spells
- **Strengths**: Early pressure, board clear (Eruption), hasty finishers
- **Weaknesses**: Low creature durability, runs out of steam without refill
- **Key cards**: Ember Sprite (1-cost swift), Magma Golem (3/4 blast), Dragon Whelp (5/4 blast)

### Water — Draw & Defense
- **Identity**: Card advantage (draw keyword), walls, healing
- **Strengths**: Never runs out of cards, durable blockers, sustain
- **Weaknesses**: Low attack power, slow to pressure, vulnerable to AoE
- **Key cards**: Tide Sprite (1/2 draw), Storm Turtle (2/5 heal), Splash (1-mana draw 2)

### Earth — Big Bodies & Buffs
- **Identity**: Highest stats-per-mana creatures, Growth buff spell
- **Strengths**: Dominates board in prolonged games, efficient trades
- **Weaknesses**: Slow early game, vulnerable to tempo, weak removal
- **Key cards**: Treant Sapling (2/5 at 3), Crystal Stag (3/5 draw), Mountain Giant (4/6)

### Air — Speed & Disruption
- **Identity**: Swift creatures, bounce effects, blast chip damage
- **Strengths**: Swift bypasses blockers, tempo from bounce, flexible
- **Weaknesses**: Fragile creatures (many 1-health), poor late-game bodies
- **Key cards**: Breeze Fairy (1/1 swift draw), Wind Hawk (3/1 swift), Sky Drake (4/3 swift)

### Shadow — Removal & Attrition
- **Identity**: Targeted removal (Dark Bolt, Doom), ETB damage, drain
- **Strengths**: Removes key threats efficiently, Life Drain 2-for-1s
- **Weaknesses**: Creature stats slightly below curve, expensive removal
- **Key cards**: Sneaky Cat (2/1 swift), Ghost Knight (3/3 ETB damage), Shadow Dragon (5/5 ETB)

## Design Heuristics

1. **Cantrip creatures are safe value**: ETB draw smooths draws without being explosive
2. **Walls punish pure aggro**: 0-attack walls at 1 mana trade with 4-5 mana attackers
3. **Swift is the best keyword**: in defender-chooses combat, guaranteed damage is king
4. **AoE must be priced carefully**: Eruption at 3 mana defines the aggro/midrange boundary
5. **Draw 2 at 1 mana is intentionally pushed**: Splash is Water's identity card
6. **Unconditional removal deserves premium**: Doom at 4 mana is correct; Dark Bolt at 1 for 2 damage is the budget option
7. **Life gain is filler**: pure heal spells should be cheap or come with other effects
8. **5-drops need impact**: at 5 mana, the creature must threaten to end the game
